## What this establishes

Existing work establishes that medication words can be poorly represented by aggregate WER, prior systems have used synthetic speech for medication vocabulary recognition, accent and domain shifts matter, phonetic information can aid entity correction, and FDA POCA screens name similarity. These are established ingredients, so none should be presented as the novelty by itself.

## Preliminary research gap for the pivoted study

The pivoted research gap is narrower: compare audio-aware medication-error prioritization against confidence, recognizer disagreement, vocabulary anomaly, and N-best uncertainty on original human speech with speaker and ingredient holdouts, then test external transfer from AfriSpeech read-sentence speech to PriMock57 mock conversations. Existing medication ASR work motivates the entity-specific target; selective ASR and OOD/error-detection work motivates the baselines and review-budget framing. The project must establish the precise closest-work comparison before claiming novelty.

This is a **provisional gap statement**, not a priority or first-of-its-kind claim. The literature search must expand across medical ASR, medication-name error correction, clinical dictation post-processing, selective prediction/conformal risk control, phonetic drug-name similarity, cross-corpus speech evaluation, and 2025–2026 work. ## Difference from POCA and correction tools

- **POCA** compares drug-name strings for orthographic and phonetic similarity in FDA’s proprietary-name review process. It does not take an audio recording or ASR hypothesis as input, and its score is not the probability that a particular recognizer will make a substitution. MISHEAR uses transparent published baselines and may report POCA only for reproducibly obtained scores; it will not infer POCA scores from its own metrics.
- **ASR entity-correction tools** generally attempt to replace or repair a recognizer’s output using entity catalogs, phonetic features, or language models. MISHEAR’s principal contribution is framed as prediction and evaluation, including a separate audit decision that can flag or abstain without automatically changing the medication token. Suggested alternatives are evaluated after the auditor predicts.
- **Medication ASR adaptation work** such as SynthASR modifies the recognizer to improve recognition. MISHEAR instead evaluates frozen recognizers and asks whether error vulnerability can be predicted across ingredients, acoustic conditions, and recognizer architectures. This does not imply that adaptation is unhelpful; it is a different question.
