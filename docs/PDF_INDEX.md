# Historical blueprint index

The original “MISHEAR v2 Complete Execution Plan” described a synthetic TTS/DSP medication-ASR benchmark. That protocol and its derived pilot outputs are now **superseded** by the cross-corpus human-speech protocol in [`../prespecification.md`](../prespecification.md).

The active question compares an audio-aware medication transcription auditor with confidence and recognizer-disagreement baselines on held-out speakers and medication ingredients, then evaluates transfer to PriMock57. Active corpus, blinding, split, and review-budget rules are documented in [`DATA_AND_LICENSING.md`](DATA_AND_LICENSING.md), [`PHASE4_SPLITS_AND_ALIGNMENT.md`](PHASE4_SPLITS_AND_ALIGNMENT.md), and [`DAVIDSON_TECHNICAL_PAPER.md`](DAVIDSON_TECHNICAL_PAPER.md).

Historical source details remain useful for provenance only. Synthetic voice settings, DSP conditions, pair-level H1–H4 endpoints, and Phase 6 pilot numbers do not answer the current human-speech research question.
